Privacy Policy

Hermes Gmail Calendar Integration

Data accessed

The current version may access Gmail messages, Gmail account metadata, Google Calendar information, and basic Google identity information authorized by the account owner.

Purpose

Data is accessed only to fulfill the account owner’s requests through their personal Hermes Agent installation.

Permissions

The current version requests read-only Gmail and Calendar permissions. Future versions may request additional permissions to create, update, or delete calendar information or to send or manage email. Additional permissions will require separate authorization by the account owner before use.

Processing and storage

Retrieved information is processed by the owner’s Hermes installation and its configured AI service providers. Information may appear in conversation history and local logs controlled by the owner.

Sharing

The integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Information is disclosed only to service providers configured by the account owner as necessary to fulfill the owner’s requests.

Revocation

The account owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account’s third-party connections settings.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated before materially different uses of Google user data or additional account-management capabilities are introduced.

Contact

Contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
